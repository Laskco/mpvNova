mpvNova Audio Filters
Version: 2026-06-08

This document lists the actual mpv/FFmpeg lavfi filter chains used by the
player audio controls.

DRC VOICE (added 2026-09-05)
==========================

An optional third DRC preset, after DRC and DRC+. Existing preset indices,
saved selections, defaults, and the original two chains are unchanged.

    optional Volume Boost -> aresample -> normal DRC dynaudnorm ->
    bass reduction -> speech presence EQ -> compressor with makeup gain -> limiter

    dynaudnorm=f=100:p=1/sqrt(2):m=100:s=12:g=11,
    bass=g=-9:f=180:t=q:w=0.7,
    equalizer=f=2400:t=q:w=0.8:g=2.5,
    acompressor=threshold=0.18:ratio=4:attack=5:release=220:makeup=2.4:knee=2.8:detection=rms:link=maximum,
    alimiter=limit=0.90:attack=5:release=80:level=false

The normal DRC quiet-passage lift is retained. Bass is reduced by up to 9 dB,
speech presence around 2.4 kHz is boosted by 2.5 dB, and linked compression
reduces louder passages with explicit makeup gain to avoid an overly quiet
result. The limiter does not automatically boost its output back to 0 dBFS.
Channels remain coupled; this does not force stereo or alter channel mapping.

The speech EQ and compressor makeup gain were reduced from the initial
4 dB / 3.0x values after listening feedback reported overly loud, harsh voices.
Timing, compression ratio, bass reduction, and the limiter ceiling are unchanged.

DRC Voice High is appended after DRC Voice and restores that initial tuning
exactly: speech EQ gain 4 dB and compressor makeup 3.0x. Everything else in
the Voice chain, including the optional post-Voice-Boost limiter, is identical.
DRC Voice remains the gentler version at its existing saved index (3); High
uses index 4 and must be selected explicitly. Neither changes the defaults.

The usual DRC rules below also apply to DRC Voice: Audio Normalization is
mutually exclusive, and user-selected Channel Downmix, Center Boost, Volume
Boost, and Voice Boost remain available. With additional Voice Boost, a second
peak limiter follows that EQ. Unlike DRC+, it never enables codec-level E-AC3
DRC, so its compressor is the same for every decoded audio format.

This is dynamics/EQ processing, not dialogue separation: music and speech
sharing the same channels/frequencies cannot be independently controlled.
Passthrough audio cannot be processed by decoded-audio filters.


FILTER ORDER
============

mpvNova builds one mpv `af` chain from left to right. The left-most filter runs
first, then each later filter receives the output of the previous one.

When DRC or DRC+ is ON:

    Channel Downmix -> DRC/DRC+ audio stage -> DRC-specific Voice Boost

When DRC and DRC+ are OFF:

    Channel Downmix -> Center Boost -> Audio Normalization -> Voice Boost -> Volume Boost

DRC, DRC+, and Audio Normalization are mutually exclusive.

Runtime rules:

    1. Channel Downmix is always placed before dynamics filters.

       If enabled on a 5.1/7.1 source, it converts the selected surround track
       to stereo before DRC, DRC+, Audio Normalization, Voice Boost, or
       Volume Boost.

       If the source is already stereo, Channel Downmix does not add a filter.

    2. Center Boost is independent.

       Active values are numeric only:

           3.0, 3.5, 4.0, 4.5, 5.0

       When DRC or DRC+ is on, Center Boost is folded into that mode's
       `aresample` options as `center_mix_level=<value>`.

       When DRC and DRC+ are off, Center Boost adds its own `@centerboost`
       aresample stage before Audio Normalization, Voice Boost, and Volume
       Boost.

    3. DRC and DRC+ are the primary dynamics path.

       When either DRC level is on, Audio Normalization is blocked. Both
       levels use the dedicated `@drcaudio` stage:

           DRC:  optional Volume Boost multiplier -> dynaudnorm -> aresample
           DRC+: optional Volume Boost multiplier -> aresample -> limiter
                 plus compressor on non-E-AC3 tracks

       Voice Boost can still run after DRC/DRC+, but it uses the DRC-specific
       Voice Boost preset list.

    4. Volume Boost behaves differently depending on DRC/DRC+/Audio Normalization.

       When DRC or DRC+ is on, Volume Boost is folded into the `@drcaudio`
       stage as a linear `volume=<multiplier>` filter.

       When Audio Normalization is on, Volume Boost is appended after Audio
       Normalization with a limiter.

       When none of DRC, DRC+, or Audio Normalization is on, Volume Boost uses
       its full compressor -> volume -> limiter chain.

    5. Audio Normalization is the alternate dynamics path.

       When Audio Normalization is on, DRC and DRC+ are blocked. The chain can
       still use Channel Downmix before it, then Voice Boost and Volume Boost
       after it.

    5. Persist Filters does not add audio filters.

       It only saves the current filter levels for the next file.

Example chains:

    DRC only:

        @drcaudio:lavfi=[dynaudnorm=...,aresample=...]

    DRC + Volume Boost:

        @drcaudio:lavfi=[volume=<multiplier>,dynaudnorm=...,aresample=...]

    Center Boost:

        @centerboost:lavfi=[
            aresample=<rate>:in_chlayout=<layout>:out_chlayout=<layout>:out_sample_fmt=<format>:center_mix_level=<value>
        ]

    DRC+:

        @drcaudio:lavfi=[
            aresample=...,
            acompressor=threshold=0.025:ratio=10:attack=3:release=130:makeup=2.3:knee=2:detection=peak:link=maximum,
            alimiter=limit=0.95:attack=5:release=50:level=false,
            aformat=sample_fmts=<format>
        ]

    DRC+ with E-AC3:

        ad-lavc-ac3drc=0.8
        @drcaudio:lavfi=[
            aresample=...,
            alimiter=limit=0.95:attack=5:release=50:level=false,
            aformat=sample_fmts=<format>
        ]

    DRC + Channel Downmix + Voice Boost:

        @dialoguedownmix:lavfi=[pan=stereo|...],
        @drcaudio:lavfi=[dynaudnorm=...,aresample=...],
        @voiceboost:lavfi=[DRC-specific EQ preset]

    Audio Normalization + Voice Boost + Volume Boost:

        @dynaudnorm:lavfi=[normalization preset],
        @voiceboost:lavfi=[normal EQ preset],
        @volumeboost:lavfi=[volume=<dB>dB,alimiter=...]

    Channel Downmix + Audio Normalization + Voice Boost + Volume Boost:

        @dialoguedownmix:lavfi=[pan=stereo|...],
        @dynaudnorm:lavfi=[normalization preset],
        @voiceboost:lavfi=[normal EQ preset],
        @volumeboost:lavfi=[volume=<dB>dB,alimiter=...]


DRC
===

Core DRC body:

    dynaudnorm=f=100:p=1/sqrt(2):m=100:s=12:g=11

Runtime DRC stage:

    @drcaudio:lavfi=[
        volume=<linear multiplier if Volume Boost is on>,
        dynaudnorm=f=100:p=1/sqrt(2):m=100:s=12:g=11,
        aresample=<rate>:in_chlayout=<layout>:out_chlayout=<layout>:out_sample_fmt=<format>[:center_mix_level=<value>]
    ]

If Channel Downmix is active on a 5.1/7.1 source, Channel Downmix feeds DRC
first and DRC treats the input/output layout as stereo.


DRC+
====

DRC+ is the Vimu-derived DRC mode recovered from Vimu's native media engine.
It is level 2 on the same DRC control. It blocks Audio Normalization the same
way regular DRC does, and it still uses the DRC-specific Voice Boost presets.

For non-E-AC3 selected audio tracks, including AC3, DRC+ builds:

    @drcaudio:lavfi=[
        volume=<linear multiplier if Volume Boost is on>,
        aresample=<rate>:in_chlayout=<layout>:out_chlayout=<layout>:out_sample_fmt=<format>[:center_mix_level=<value>],
        acompressor=threshold=0.025:ratio=10:attack=3:release=130:makeup=2.3:knee=2:detection=peak:link=maximum,
        alimiter=limit=0.95:attack=5:release=50:level=false,
        aformat=sample_fmts=<format>
    ]

For E-AC3 selected audio tracks, DRC+ skips the compressor and enables native
decoder DRC scale:

    ad-lavc-ac3drc=0.8

    @drcaudio:lavfi=[
        volume=<linear multiplier if Volume Boost is on>,
        aresample=<rate>:in_chlayout=<layout>:out_chlayout=<layout>:out_sample_fmt=<format>[:center_mix_level=<value>],
        alimiter=limit=0.95:attack=5:release=50:level=false,
        aformat=sample_fmts=<format>
    ]

When DRC+ is off, or when the selected track is not E-AC3, mpvNova sets:

    ad-lavc-ac3drc=0


CENTER BOOST
============

Center Boost is a separate audio-filter row. It has five active numeric values:

    3.0
    3.5
    4.0
    4.5
    5.0

When DRC or DRC+ is active, the selected value is folded into the existing DRC
aresample stage:

    aresample=<rate>:in_chlayout=<layout>:out_chlayout=<layout>:out_sample_fmt=<format>:center_mix_level=<value>

When DRC and DRC+ are off, Center Boost adds its own filter stage:

    @centerboost:lavfi=[
        aresample=<rate>:in_chlayout=<layout>:out_chlayout=<layout>:out_sample_fmt=<format>:center_mix_level=<value>
    ]

VOLUME BOOST
============

Available levels:

    0, +2, +4, +6, +8, +10, +12, +15, +18, +21 dB

If DRC, DRC+, or Audio Normalization is active:

    @volumeboost:lavfi=[
        volume=<dB>dB,
        alimiter=limit=<limit>:attack=2:release=20
    ]

Limiter value by boost amount:

    <= +4 dB:  0.97
    <= +8 dB:  0.95
    <= +12 dB: 0.93
    <= +15 dB: 0.91
    >  +15 dB: 0.89

If DRC and Audio Normalization are both off:

    @volumeboost:lavfi=[
        acompressor=threshold=<threshold>:ratio=<ratio>:attack=6:release=90:knee=3.0:link=average:detection=rms:makeup=1.02,
        volume=<dB>dB,
        alimiter=limit=<limit>:attack=2:release=20
    ]

Compressor/limiter values by boost amount:

    <= +4 dB:  threshold=-19dB, ratio=1.6, limit=0.95
    <= +8 dB:  threshold=-20dB, ratio=1.9, limit=0.93
    <= +12 dB: threshold=-21dB, ratio=2.2, limit=0.91
    <= +15 dB: threshold=-22dB, ratio=2.5, limit=0.89
    >  +15 dB: threshold=-23dB, ratio=2.8, limit=0.87

When DRC or DRC+ is active, Volume Boost is folded into the DRC audio stage as
a linear multiplier.
